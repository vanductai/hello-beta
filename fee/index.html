Pricing model
  • BTS comm = y% of SI agreed price (kickback from SI, default 10%)
  • SRS comm = x% of SRS sell price (default 20%). SRS sell = BTS→SRS price ÷ (1 − x%)
  • Floor price = SI + FCT + Payment fee — min BTS→SRS price to avoid loss
  • Net contract: FCT = Net ÷ (1 − r%) × r% (gross-up). Cost = SI + FCT
  • Gross contract: FCT = Gross × r% (withheld). Cost = SI price
  • BTS margin = (BTS sell − Floor) + BTS commission from SI
  • Editing SRS sell → recalcs BTS→SRS = SRS × (1 − x%) and Customer
  • Editing Customer → back-calcs SRS = Cust ÷ (1 + VAT%), then BTS→SRS
  • Exchange rate: VND conversion uses Vietcombank sell rate. Enter manually or toggle VND columns on/off.
